How a report is placed

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the report did not come through the booking-v3 form. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap. The queued fix for #4471 is this one.
  • hint: the tenant runs booking-v3 and the report came through its form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type month first as it tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this: the hint has to follow the tenant’s locale.
  • one-day-move: the desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The date lands a day off, or two people in different time zones see different days. Day and month do not swap. This is a time-zone bug with its own fix.

The cause comes from the tenant (locales.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and on #4471 it is wrong in both directions: see the “tagged as” column on the #4471 page.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. The subjects in #4471, such as “Date is wrong” and “Wrong day on the invite”, fit every one of these problems, so they all landed in one ticket. The triage page that applies the rule is marked stale.

#4471 is marked known, with one fix queued. That fix covers the parser problem only. The hint and one-day-move problems stay open until their own tickets close.

What the desk does now

  • Keep #4471 for the parser problem: the queued fix covers it.
  • Send new reports from the booking-v3 form to the hint ticket, and one-day moves to the one-day-move ticket.
  • Count each report once, by id: the export repeats some.
