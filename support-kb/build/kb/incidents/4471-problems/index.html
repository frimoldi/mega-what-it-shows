How a report is placed

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the report did not come through the booking-v3 form. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap. The fix for #4471 is this one; it shipped.
  • hint: the tenant runs booking-v3 and the report came through its form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type month first as it tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this. It won’t be fixed: the booking-v3 form is retired.
  • one-day-move: the desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The date lands a day off, or two people in different time zones see different days. Day and month do not swap. This is a time-zone bug with its own fix. The desk files these by tenant, so they stay counted under parser too.

The cause comes from the tenant (locales.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and on #4471 it is wrong in both directions: see the “tagged as” column on the #4471 page.
